15th Japan-China Go Exchange, 1987
Go World 48 writes:
In late May and early June this year an eight-man Japanese team
headed by Kato Masao toured China as the 15th in the series of
annual goodwill tours. The Japanese team won 32 of the 56 games
they played, making this the most successful visit to China
since the first tour in 1973 (when they won 40 games).
The meetings were in Beijing (5/20, 5/22, 5/24), Chongqing (5/26), Yangtze (5/28, 5/31) and Wuhan (6/3).
