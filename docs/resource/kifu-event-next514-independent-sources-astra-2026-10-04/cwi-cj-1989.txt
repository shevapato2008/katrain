17th Japan-China Go Exchange, 1989
Go World 55 writes:
This year an eight-member Japanese team of
young players whose average age was 20 toured
China for two weeks in the middle of May. The
host country also fielded young players, and by
coincidence the average age of the 41 players in
the Chinese teams was also 20. The result of the
seven matches was a narrow victory for the touring side,
which scored 29 wins to 27 losses.
The stars for the touring side were Morita 4-dan and
Miss Aoki Kikuyo 2-dan, both of whom scored 6-1.
