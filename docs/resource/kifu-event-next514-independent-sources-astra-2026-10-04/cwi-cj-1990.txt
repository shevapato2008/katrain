18th Japan-China Go Exchange, 1990
Go World 59 writes:
This year the Chinese sent a team of eight players under 28 and under 8-dan to Japan.
The defending side fielded various teams of players under 8-dan and under 26
in a series of seven matches played between 19 and 30 April.
The touring team scored 38 wins to 18 losses.
The Japanese lost one of the matches 0-8 (on 4/26), the second time this has happened.
Individual best-of-three matches were held in the 5th to 7th rounds of the tour,
and here the Chinese won six out of eight.
Most successful of the tourists was Liao Guiyong 7-dan, who won all of his games.
Two players had 6-1 records: Shao 4-dan and Zhang Xuan 7-dan.
Zhang is one of the top women players in China.
Another woman who did well, with 5-2, is Yang Hui 7-dan, the wife of Tsao 9-dan.
