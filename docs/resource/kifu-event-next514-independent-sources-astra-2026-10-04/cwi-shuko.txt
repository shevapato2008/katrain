1984 - 4th Fujisawa tour
Nine Japanese players toured China and played various opponents.
Go World 36 writes:
This year, for the fourth year in a row, Fujisawa Shuko made a private tour of China, taking with
him a group of promising young professionals. ... The group this year consisted of
nine young professionals, including Kiyonari Tetsuya 8-dan (age 23), Yoda Norimoto 5-dan
(age 18) and Shuko's son, Kazunari 2-dan (age 19). ...
The team played four matches against Chinese teams, including one all-woman team,
and scored 16 wins to 20 losses.
