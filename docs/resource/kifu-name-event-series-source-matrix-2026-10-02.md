## Grouping guardrails

1. Split a recognized leading ordinal into an edition field only after the residual event name maps to a documented series. Do not strip words such as `Old`, `Japan`, sponsor, league level, or geography.
2. Keep Old Meijin distinct from modern Meijin. Nihon Ki-in's historical record uses 旧・名人戦 and its current Meijin archive is a separate series; the CWI record demonstrates the transition from 14th Old Meijin to 1st Meijin in 1974–75.
3. Treat localized “Ryusei” as geography-bound. The Chinese raw strings explicitly say Japan, while the source also documents Japan–China Ryusei matches separately.
4. Keep tournament identity separate from sponsor, division, season, and stage. Korean league evidence supports a league family but not merging its A/B divisions or sponsor-era labels.
5. A supported core grouping is not translation approval. For all eleven display languages, retain pending status until the language-specific form has direct evidence or an approved editorial decision.
