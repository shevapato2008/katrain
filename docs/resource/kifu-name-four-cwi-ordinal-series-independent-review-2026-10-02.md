## Series boundaries and edition evidence

- **Oza.** The [Nihon Ki-in Oza archive](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/index.html) has one numbered 王座戦 history and explicitly lists the 26th title in 1978 and the 28th in 1980. The [CWI Oza title index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Oza/) and [26th-edition page](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Oza/26/index.html) agree on those edition years and distinguish tournament games from title games. The direct rows' exact `Oza/<matching edition>/` path and `EV` support one Oza series with separate edition and stage.
- **Old Meijin.** Nihon Ki-in's [historical result sheet](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/z-obizaka/7final.pdf) uses `旧・名人戦` for its 12th–14th editions. The [CWI Old Meijin index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/OMeijin/) lists editions 1–14 and its [14th-edition page](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/OMeijin/14/index.html) records the 1976 sponsor change and numbering restart. Thus these rows remain **Old Meijin**, separate from the later Asahi Meijin series; early preliminary dates can precede the title year.
- **Pro Best Ten.** The [CWI edition/results index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/ProBest10/) gives one Asahi professional tournament with editions 1–12 and separate playoff/ranking games. Nihon Ki-in's [Cho Chikun record](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000004.htm) independently names his 12th-edition プロ十傑戦 title. Keep the professional series separate from amateur or student 十傑 events, and retain playoff stages.
- **Nihon Ki-in Championship.** The [CWI championship index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/NKCh/) lists editions 1–22 as 日本棋院選手権戦; its [17th](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/NKCh/17/index.html) and [22nd](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/NKCh/22/index.html) pages show preliminary games in the year before their title matches. Nihon Ki-in's [historical results](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/docs/5renpa_20100825.pdf) separately name 日本棋院選手権戦, 日本棋院第一位決定戦, 全日本第一位決定戦, and 天元戦; its [Sakata](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000006.htm) and [Kato](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000002.html) records identify the 20th and 21st Championship editions. Tengen's succession after the 22nd edition does not join it to this identity.

## Oza ordinal conflict: HOLD 17

Album IDs **170812–170828** are exactly `Oza/26/P01.sgf` through `P17.sgf`. All 17 complete source SGFs say `EV[28th Oza]` and `RO[3rd Preliminary]`; their dates run **1977-12-15 through 1978-05-11**. The source path says edition 26. Nihon Ki-in and CWI place the 26th title in 1978 and the 28th in 1980; CWI's [Cho Chikun Oza index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Cho_Chikun/tournaments/Oza.html) also shows a 28th preliminary game in 1979. The path and dates suggest these 17 may be 26th-edition preliminaries with a repeated `EV` error, but neither the title histories nor the sampled bodies independently identify every game. **Do not silently change `28th` to `26th`, assign an edition, or add these rows to the approved cohort.** Resolve them through independent game-level or contemporary bracket evidence, then review each row's edition explicitly.

The other **128 held rows** occur outside their matching numbered series directory: 35 Oza, 15 Old Meijin, 46 Pro Best Ten, and 32 Nihon Ki-in Championship. Some may truly belong to these series, but their exact source paths do not give this review's directory corroboration. They require separate row-level source verification. The 32 Championship holds include edition values 1 and 4–7, which have **no** direct-path approved rows; absence here is not a judgment that those editions are outside the historical series.
