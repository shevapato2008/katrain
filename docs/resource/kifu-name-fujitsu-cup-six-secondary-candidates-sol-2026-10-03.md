# Fujitsu Cup: six secondary-language display candidates

**Status:** unsigned source research and finite candidates, 2026-10-03 (Asia/Shanghai). This extends the [five-language independent display review](kifu-name-fujitsu-cup-five-independent-review-2026-10-03.md) only as research. It approves no new language display, event identity, album/SGF link, foreign key, or database write.

The intended event is the historic professional [Nihon Ki-in Fujitsu Cup series](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/fujitsu/index-e.html), 24 editions from 1988 through 2011. The publisher archive is the common identity comparator, not a source for the six target-language spellings. I captured five publisher HTTP bodies and two Ukrainian component-basis bodies, with URL, UTC capture time, byte count, SHA-256, and short exact passage in the protected source manifest. The Russian Go Library page resisted direct HTTP retrieval; its search-engine-rendered excerpt is retained and explicitly distinguished from a raw page body.

| Language | Candidate | Evidence and same-event check | Candidate status |
|---|---|---|---|
| German | `Fujitsu Cup` | The [Deutscher Go-Bund national-team page](https://www.dgob.de/organisation/spitzensport/nationalmannschaft/) lists it among two professional world-championship participations, separately from LG Cup. | Source-use candidate; independent review pending. |
| Spanish | `Copa Fujitsu` | The [Asociación Argentina de Go international-participation page](https://go.org.ar/index.php/torneos-y-actividades/participacion-internacional) calls it a professional tournament in Japan; its 2011 edition 24 and Park Jeong-Hwan winner match the Nihon Ki-in archive. | Source-use candidate; independent review pending. |
| French | `Coupe Fujitsu` | The [Belgian Go Federation Belgo scan](https://www.gofed.be/files/belgo%2058.pdf), PDF page 20, calls the third cup a world championship and mentions Takemiya's first two wins. I checked the page image against OCR. The **European Grand Prix Fujitsu** on that same page is a separate circuit and is excluded. | Source-use candidate; independent review pending. |
| Russian | `Кубок Фудзицу` | The [Russian Go Library tournament page](https://rusgolib.gofederation.ru/FujitsuCup) search-rendered excerpt identifies the first world Go championship in 1988 and Fujitsu sponsorship. Direct local HTTPS/HTTP fetch timed out, so the publisher's raw body and hash are unavailable. | Candidate pending raw-body capture and independent review. |
| Turkish | `Fujitsu Kupası` | The [İstanbul Go Okulu Cho Chikun account](https://www.gookulu.com/cho_chikun/) uses inflected `Fujitsu Kupası’nı`; removing the accusative suffix gives the nominative candidate. Its 1991 Cho Chikun–Qian Yuping final matches Nihon Ki-in edition 4. | Inflected-use-derived candidate; independent review pending. |
| Ukrainian | `Кубок Фудзіцу` | **No use of the full event label was located** in bounded Ukrainian Go searches. [Ukrainian Go Federation](https://ufgo.org/%D0%BA%D1%83%D0%B1%D0%BE%D0%BA-%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%97%D0%BD%D0%B8-%D0%B7-%D2%91%D0%BE-2001/) uses `Кубок` for a Go competition; [KPI conference proceedings](https://history.kpi.ua/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Conference-proceedings-2024.pdf), PDF page 56, print `«Фудзіцу» («Fujitsu»)`. Combining these is a proposed construction, not an attested Fujitsu Cup name. | **HOLD: rule-based proposal only.** |

Searches for Ukrainian `Кубок Фудзіцу`, `Фудзіцу` with Go terms, `Фуджитсу` with Go/cup terms, and the federation's site found no credible historical-event use. That bounded negative result cannot establish that no such usage exists elsewhere.
