# Gu Li and Lee Sedol: 11-language source matrix

Research date: 2026-10-02. Scope: source and identity research only. The approximate slot counts in the assignment are inventory context, not player identity evidence or authorization to bind any slot. No database IDs, candidates, approvals, or writes are made here.

## Identity anchors

* **Gu Li (古力):** The Chinese Chess and Card Games Administration Center profile [“古力”](https://www.sport.gov.cn/qpzx/n27319120/c27440754/content.html) is a visible Simplified Chinese page. It identifies him as a Chinese professional Go 9-dan, Chongqing Go team head coach since 2017, learned Go at six, entered the professional ranks in 1995, and studied under Nie Weiping from late 1997. The same official center's [account of Gu Li and Lee Sedol](https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20745751/n20767274/c22259758/content.html) states both were born in 1983 and entered professional ranks in 1995. These details identify the Go player rather than other people named Gu Li.
* **Lee Sedol (이세돌):** The Korean Baduk Association [player profile](https://m.baduk.or.kr/record/gisa_info.asp?PRPL_CODE=10000119) is a visible Korean page. It states `9단 이세돌 (李世乭)`, birth `1983년 03월 02일`, affiliation Korea, pupil of Kwon Kap-yong, and records his professional history and AlphaGo match. The official Chinese Chess and Card Games Administration Center article above independently describes Lee as a Korean Go player, born in 1983, who turned professional in 1995 and played the famous rivalry with Gu Li.

Pages were read in rendered browser text/search extracts during this pass; no raw response archive or body hashes were produced. Search-result extracts are labeled as leads below and are not treated as inspected full article bodies. No source mirrors are counted as independent evidence.

## Disposition and unresolved work

The sources establish distinct people and several directly observed target-language spellings, but do not close all eleven language scopes. The strongest identity anchors are the Chinese national sports administration for Gu Li and Korea Baduk Association for Lee Sedol. The Chinese-language Lee Sedol spelling conflict (`李世石` / `李世乭`) is substantive and remains unresolved by locale. The French Gu Li page has incorrect birth-year and pro-entry details compared with the official Chinese profile, so those fields must not be reused. German, Spanish, Turkish, and Ukrainian Gu Li evidence is incomplete; Turkish Lee Sedol and independent French/Spanish/Russian Lee Sedol Go-publication checks remain outstanding. “Not found in this pass” is a scope note, not evidence that a language lacks a conventional form.
