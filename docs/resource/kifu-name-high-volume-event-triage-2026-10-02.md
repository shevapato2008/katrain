## Source findings

- GNU Project describes GNU Go as a Go-playing program and specifically states GNU Go 3.8 was released on 2009-02-19: [GNU Go](https://www.gnu.org/software/gnugo/). This supports the program-label classification, while making the much earlier SGF dates a genuine provenance anomaly.
- The CWI specialist archive's [Hoensha page](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Hoensha/) identifies 方円社 as a professional Go organization founded in 1879 and says records from its monthly meetings appeared in *Igo Shinpo*. That supports an organization/archive context, not a named tournament identity.
- Nihon Ki-in's [Go history page](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/history/04.html) describes 御城碁 as annual official games at Edo Castle, beginning in 1626 and totaling about 530 games through 1861. Its [Shusaku history](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/teach/history/history02.html) also treats Castle Games as a repeated historical series. CWI's [Dosaku archive](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Dosaku/) lists preserved games under “Castle games.” Together these are strong evidence that the phrase has a real series referent, but not evidence that every row in the current SGF bucket is accurately dated or belongs to it.
- The Chinese Go Association rules define dan rank as a player rank system, and official Chinese sports administration pages use “national Go Dan Tournament” for a particular competition ([rules](https://cwqa.qilue.com/rules/), [National Go Dan Tournament report](https://www.sport.gov.cn/n14471/n14482/n14519/c737737/content.html)). This illustrates why an unqualified `段位赛` should remain generic unless a source ties a game to a specific competition.
- The State General Administration of Sport describes the National Go Individual Championship as a named event founded in 1957 ([2018 report](https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20067662/n20067613/c22762310/content.html)); the raw value `个人赛` alone lacks the “national Go” qualifier and edition evidence required to link these 638 records to it.

4. **No bulk inference from context:** dates, opponents, source directory, or player identities may prioritize review but do not alone establish an event identity. Any collision, conflicting source, malformed/multi-date record, or uncertainty remains HOLD under the localization plan's evidence and independent-review requirements.

The proposed display work can cover up to 2,574 rows (2,134 description rows plus 440 series rows) without fabricating per-game tournament IDs. It is a finite triage boundary, not an approval or import batch.
