## Ukrainian closure

The shortest positive route is a dated Ukrainian-language Go source or identifiable Ukrainian specialist publication that names **the Japanese professional Honinbo tournament**, preferably with an edition, sponsor, or Nihon Ki-in link, and uses a displayable event phrase. A Ukrainian source about `Київський Хон'імбо`, an honorary Honinbo, Shusaku, or the Edo house would not close this gap. Compare any found spelling with the Ukrainian Wikipedia `Хон'імбо` and record why the selected form is preferred.

If no credible positive event form is found, the [localization design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-kifu-full-name-localization-design.md) permits a **generated** form only after a bounded, logged negative search across a target-language Go specialist source and a target-language encyclopedia or exact Wikidata language field, including known leads and search limits. A possible **unapproved** explanatory wording is `Турнір Хон'імбо`, using the source-attested Ukrainian proper-name spelling plus the ordinary Ukrainian event noun. It is neither the exact Wikipedia phrase nor a proven convention. Any generated candidate still needs its own Ukrainian editorial check, rule/provenance record, and independent controlled-candidate review. This memo creates and approves no such candidate.
