# Honinbo edition wording: source research (2026-10-02)

Scope: source research for display wording on the 1st, 17th, and 34th Honinbo editions in the eleven supported locales. This memo records observations and generated-rule proposals only. It does not approve candidates, alter the signed Honinbo bundle, or establish raw-event selections.

## Evidence and limits

- The [Nihon Ki-in historical archive](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/honinbo/archive.html) labels its edition column `期` and records the 34th edition as `34期` (1979). The Go-specialist [Igo Kifu page for the 34th edition](https://igo-kifu.com/event/honinbo/34) gives the full title `第34期本因坊戦挑戦手合七番勝負`. Japanese therefore has direct series-specific evidence for prefix `第N期` and no `回`.
- The CWI Go game archive directly titles the samples [“The 1st Honinbo title match”](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Honinbo/01/index.html), [“17th Honinbo title”](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Honinbo/17/index.html), and [“34th Honinbo title”](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Honinbo/34/index.html). These are observed English forms, although they are page titles rather than Nihon Ki-in branding.
- The mainland Go record index [Kihuu](https://www.kihuu.net/index.php?key=%E5%9D%82%E7%94%B0%E6%A0%84%E7%94%B7&type=2) contains both `第17期日本本因坊战决赛第一局` and `第17届日本本因坊战`. The competing `期`/`届` forms show that Chinese usage is not uniform. Use `期` for this Japan-specific series because it preserves the unit used by the Japanese organizer and is itself attested in Go-game records; `第N期本因坊战/戰` remains a generated localized composition, not an authoritative complete label.
- The Korean Go history [BaDukWorld](https://www.badukworld.co.kr/biz/lesson1/history1/hon23.html) describes the 1962 and 1963 events using `第17期 本因坊戰` and `第18期 本因坊戰` inside Korean prose. It corroborates the source unit, but it retains Japanese kanji; it does not attest the Hangul composition `제N기 본인방전`.
- German Wikipedia's [Masao Katō results](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masao_Kat%C5%8D) lists `1979 34. Honinbo`, an observed German-context shorthand for that edition. Its [Hon’inbō article](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hon%E2%80%99inb%C5%8D_%28Turnier%29) calls the series a `Turnier` but does not supply 1st/17th/34th full edition labels.
- No series-specific complete edition label was located for Spanish, French, Russian, Turkish, or Ukrainian. French Go site [Jeu de go](https://jeudego.org/_php/rejoue_partie.php?s=ceocpieiqhlj) uses `34e Honinbo féminin` for the separate women's series; this is adjacent ordinal syntax evidence only, not evidence for the open Honinbo event.

No evidence found in this sample attests complete localized labels for all three representative editions in Chinese, Korean, German, Spanish, French, Russian, Turkish, or Ukrainian. For these, the forms below are versioned compositions from the source-reviewed base names and locale grammar; they must remain explicitly marked generated. The prior preview's common `core · ordinal-unit` suffix is not a supported shared construction: Japanese has a prefix, German has an observed prefix shorthand, and the other locales need locale-specific order and punctuation.

## Per-locale rule proposal

`Observed` means the cited source contains that series-specific form. `Generated` means composition from the verified base name and locale ordinal syntax; it is not claimed as an observed published label. The rule ID is a proposal for a later owner decision, not an approval.

These rules yield potentially awkward repetition in Spanish/French (`edición/édition` + a base that already says `Torneo/Tournoi`) and explicit noun repetition in Turkish/Ukrainian. They are conservative compositional drafts, not evidence that local Go writers use those exact strings. A raw-event owner should approve locale wording and, where desired, select a source-backed shorter style before any import.

## Review and import boundary

The already reviewed series bases are: `Honinbo`, `本因坊战`, `本因坊戰`, `本因坊戦`, `본인방전`, `Hon’inbō`, `Torneo Hon'inbō`, `Tournoi Hon'inbō`, `Турнир Хонинбо`, `Honinbo Turnuvası`, and `Турнір на звання Хон'імбо` (locale order en/cn/tw/jp/ko/de/es/fr/ru/tr/ua). 1. Review and approve each generated locale rule and its 1st/17th/34th rendered samples; the base-name approval does not imply edition wording approval.
2. Generate all 34 ordinal forms from the approved locale rules, then inspect collisions and length limits against the existing preview data.
3. Record exact 34 raw strings × 11 display values in the raw-event approval/import artifact and obtain the required `strict_slot_approvals` for the exact raw event values.
