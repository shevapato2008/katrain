## Identity check

The [Nihon Ki-in player profile](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000385.html) gives `井山 裕太（イヤマ ユウタ / IYAMA, Yuta）`, born 1989-05-24, from Higashiosaka, student of Kunio Ishii, professional entry in 2002, and 9 dan from 2009. The independent [Olympic Council of Asia athlete page](https://www.ocagames.com/HZ_Info/AG2022-/en/results/go/athlete-profile-n2014200-iyama-yuta.htm) identifies `IYAMA Yuta`, Go, Japan, date of birth 24 May 1989, professional since 2002, and Asian Games team medal results. These align on person, birth date, and career. The [China Sports Administration match report](https://www.sport.gov.cn/n20001280/n20745751/n20767274/c22143892/content.html) independently identifies Japan's 井山裕太 in a World Go Mingren final against Chen Yaoye. This verifies the intended directory identity at the person level; it does not verify every one of the 1,276 imported raw slots.
