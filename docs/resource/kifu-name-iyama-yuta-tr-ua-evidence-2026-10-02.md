# 井山裕太: Turkish and Ukrainian name evidence addendum

Research memo, 2026-10-02. This supplements the [11-language source matrix](kifu-name-iyama-yuta-source-matrix-2026-10-02.md). It records witnessed target-language usage only; it does not approve a display name, link any individual imported slot, or authorize a database write.

## Identity anchor

The [Nihon Ki-in profile](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000385.html) identifies the player as `井山 裕太（イヤマ ユウタ / IYAMA, Yuta）`, born 1989-05-24, professional since 2002, and 9 dan since 2009. The Turkish source calls `Iyama Yuta (9p)` a top-level Go player; the Ukrainian source places `Іяма Юта` / `Юта Іяма` in the 2008–2013 Japanese Meijin title table. The name, professional rank, and Meijin results fit that identity. Neither source supplies a separate birth date or kanji, so this is a contextual identity match, not independent full biographical verification.

## Search scope and cross-language check

Searches covered the exact romanized, Turkish dotted-I, and Ukrainian Cyrillic variants; Turkish Go Association (`tgod.org.tr`) and Ukrainian Go Federation (`ufgo.org`) domains; and Turkish/Ukrainian encyclopedia and Go pages. No inspectable player-specific passage was found on those two federation domains. English text under a `/tr/` URL and automatically translated Reddit pages were excluded as target-language evidence. This bounded search does not establish that federation usage is absent.

Across the other nine rows in the source matrix, no conflicting player identity or incompatible base name was found. English, German, Spanish, French, and Russian sources vary in name order and/or romanization marks; those witnessed variants should remain separate. The Turkish `Iyama Yuta` matches the Nihon Ki-in romanization in surname-first order, while Ukrainian Cyrillic is language-specific and should not be treated as a conflict with Russian `Ияма`.
