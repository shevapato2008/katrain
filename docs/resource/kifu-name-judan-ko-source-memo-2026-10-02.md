## Source-level recommendation

`일본 십단전` is an attested Korean candidate for the Japanese recurring series. `일본십단전` is also attested, including in the same 한국기원 player record and repeatedly in 사이버오로; the space is editorial variation. The `일본` qualifier disambiguates this series from Korea's separate [원익배 십단전](https://m.baduk.or.kr/record/gisa_info.asp?PRPL_CODE=10000929), which 한국기원 also records. Bare `십단전` is used in Japanese-event context but is ambiguous without that context. Keep `제51기` and other edition numbers as edition data, and keep the holder title `십단` distinct from the series name.

This recommendation is evidence for later product-language review only; no canonical DB display name is approved here.
