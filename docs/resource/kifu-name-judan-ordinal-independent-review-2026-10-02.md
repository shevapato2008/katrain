## Independent series and edition history

The [Nihon Ki-in Japanese Judan archive](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/jyudan/index.html) calls the event `十段戦`, identifies its 1961 founding, and lists terms **1 through 18** in one history, with title years 1962, 1963, 1964, 1965, 1966, 1968, 1968, 1969, 1971, 1972, 1973, 1974, 1975, 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979, and 1980. Its note explains that title-match timing changed across the early editions and later moved to March–April. The [Nihon Ki-in English history](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/jyudan/index-e.html) confirms the same numbered series. This official series identity and edition sequence are independent of the CWI SGF publication chain.

The [CWI Judan index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Judan/) separates title games from its per-edition tournament subpages. Its [1st-edition page](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Judan/01/index.html) shows 1961–62 tournament stages before the 1962 title, its [9th-edition page](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Judan/09/index.html) shows 1970 tournament games before the 1971 title, and its [16th](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Judan/16/index.html), [17th](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Judan/17/index.html), and [18th](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/Judan/18/index.html) pages show the later edition sequence. Thus an edition's source game year need not equal its official title year, especially for preliminaries. CWI pages corroborate the source publication's stage structure; they are not independent game-level proof because they share the archive's publisher.

The 39 held rows consist of 27 `CWI_1950_1978/Cho_Chikun`, nine Go Seigen paths (six `Go_Seigen`, three `CWI_History_Full/Go_Seigen`), and three `CWI_1950_1978/unusual/illegal_ko_*` paths. Their root wording alone does not meet this review's direct-directory corroboration. They require per-row source and game evidence before addition to any identity-bound batch.
