## Identity boundary

The primary identity anchor is Nihon Ki-in: its current domestic Go tournament index lists 棋聖戦 under domestic Go tournaments, and its edition archive states the organizers and 1976 founding year. The same kanji is also used for the Japan Shogi Association's ヒューリック杯棋聖戦; that shogi event is a different competition. Chinese-language evidence explicitly warns of separate Chinese and Korean Go 棋聖戰 contests, while Japan also has 女流棋聖戦 (women's Kisei), a distinct domestic series. Do not assign bare “Kisei” or 棋聖戦/棋圣战 records without Go/Japan/event context when source provenance could point to one of these other competitions.

No canonical IDs, translations, aliases, database records, staging data, or code were changed. No final naming approval is asserted.
