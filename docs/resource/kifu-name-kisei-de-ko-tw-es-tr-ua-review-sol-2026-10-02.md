## Identity and source-policy boundary

The [Nihon Ki-in event archive](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/kisei/archive.html) anchors the series and its editions. The word `Kisei` alone is unsafe: Japan also has a separate [Women's Kisei Go event](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/fkisei/), while the [Japan Shogi Association's Kisei](https://www.shogi.or.jp/match/kisei/) is shogi. Chinese and Korean Go have their own same-name competitions. Match an event only with Japanese professional Go, edition, players, organizers, dates, or equivalent provenance; a matching bare label does not establish identity.

The [approved localization design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-kifu-full-name-localization-design.md) permits source-attested names to proceed to independent review without pointless exhaustive negative searches. Generated names require a recorded reasonable target-language search and separate language review; the [live source registry](kifu-name-source-registry.json) still marks every relevant language scope `complete_for_negative_claims: false`. The positive `de`, `ko`, `tw`, and `es` forms above are source proposals, not automatically approved names. The `tr` and `ua` renderings are transparent editorial drafts only, with their search gaps explicit. This pass did not change code, staging data, database records, or canonical names.
