## Source checks and limitations

- **Organizer identity evidence:** The Korea Baduk Association's [2012 closing report](https://m.baduk.or.kr/news/B01_view.asp?news_no=620) says `KB국민은행 2012 한국바둑리그` and separately `락스타리그`, calling the latter “퓨처스리그격” (Futures-League-like). Its [2012 media-day report](https://m.baduk.or.kr/news/B01_view.asp?news_no=589) documents main-league playoff teams and season structure. Contemporary [Sports Kyunghyang coverage](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/201203192021083) names `KB국민은행 2012 락스타리그` and explicitly says “2부 리그격” (second-tier-like). This supports two distinct season identities and the sponsor only for Korean source titles.
- **Chinese-tier corroboration:** The [101围棋 archive catalogue](https://www.101weiqi.com/chessbook/player/53/) shows dated 2012 game entries under both exact Chinese labels, including main and lower tier. A 2012 [CCTV report](https://news.cntv.cn/20120415/118438.shtml) discusses a newly added Korean second division in the same season context. These sources corroborate target-language usage and tier distinction; the catalogue itself is not the organizer.
- **English main-event corroboration:** [Go to Everyone's 2012 schedule](https://gotoeveryone.k2ss.info/news/kr/kl/10/) labels the event “2012 Korean Baduk League,” says Baduk TV hosted it, and lists the season games. It does not identify a distinct Rockstar League event in the captured page.
- **Japanese main-event usage:** [Kifudepot's Japanese game archive](https://www.kifudepot.net/kifucontents.php?id=qprAR86i00uvCbuRSEltig%3D%3D) has `2012年韓国囲碁リーグ` as page heading and SGF `GN`. This is actual Japanese-language catalogue usage, not an official Japanese organizer title.
- **Secondary-language search scope:** Targeted title searches were made against the registry's German, Spanish, French, Russian, Turkish, and Ukrainian Go references, including queries for `Korean Baduk League`, the official Korean main name, and where applicable the 2012 Rockstar/lower league. Search results checked included DGOB, AEGO/GoDokoro/Chile Go Federation, FFG/Belgian Go Federation, RusGoLib/GoMagic, TGOD/Go Okulu, and UFGO. No direct local event-name usage was verified. This is a bounded research result, not a claim that no such usage exists. The proposed forms are therefore `generated`-style editorial candidates and require independent review of spelling, case, terminology, and transliteration before adoption.
- **KB sponsor boundary:** `KB국민은행` is present in the KBA season title and the exact Chinese catalog labels include `KB国民银行杯`. The direct English and Japanese usage cited here omit KB; no KB is added in those candidates. The six proposed local forms also omit KB because no target-language source establishes sponsor wording there. The sponsor may be stored as sourced metadata separately from the translated event name.
