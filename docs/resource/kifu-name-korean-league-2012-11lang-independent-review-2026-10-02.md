## Review limits and disposition

The KBA page body (Korean) says the 2012 KB Kookmin Bank Korea Baduk League had ten teams and seven months of play, and calls Rockstar League its Futures-League-like companion. The independent contemporary Sports Kyunghyang article (Korean) names `KB국민은행 2012 락스타리그`, calls it the second division, and gives separate schedules and game counts. The Chinese catalogue independently assigns the 2012 main and lower-tier games to the distinct `围甲` and `围乙` labels. The Japanese source provides an exact main-league heading and game-name field. English source provides the exact principal heading. These findings support the frozen cohort identity split.

No completed per-object/per-language `negative_closure` artifact, independent pronunciation record, or `generated_review` is supplied in the candidate matrix for the generated candidates. The matrix's bounded targeted searches and general federation/reference pages do not satisfy the runbook closure schema by themselves. Therefore none of the 16 generated candidate strings is approved. This decision does not assert that no conventional name exists outside the reviewed pages. The two Chinese candidates are rejected only for their inserted spaces; their corrected exact catalogue forms are source-attested and identity-matched. The English main candidate is likewise rejected only for `Korea` versus the page's `Korean`.

No factual conflict requiring escalation to `gpt-6-sol` arose in the reviewed source bodies. The main/lower identity distinction was consistent across Korean organizer/press evidence and the Chinese game catalogue. The Chinese 甲/乙 labels are archive usage; they are not attributed here to the Korean organizer.
