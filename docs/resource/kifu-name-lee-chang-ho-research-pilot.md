## Identity discrimination

The governing association profile is the primary identity key: Korean name 이창호, Hanja 李昌鎬, birth date 1975-07-29, Korean affiliation, rank 9 dan, and Go career. These markers distinguish him from same-name Lee Chang-ho athletes, actors, and other people in search results. The English LG Cup record independently matches the birth date, Hanja, rank, and Hangul. Tournament wins and pupil relationship to Cho Hun-hyun are useful secondary checks, not name variants.

## Research limits

- This pilot records observed conventional forms and identity evidence only. It does not approve any database candidate or settle whether UI policy prefers Hangul, Hanja, kana, or Latin forms.
- Chinese simplified and traditional spellings differ orthographically: 李昌镐 (cn) versus 李昌鎬 (tw). Both correspond to the Hanja listed by the Korean association.
- English and European languages use Lee Chang-ho / Lee Changho with variable hyphenation. Product policy should choose one consistent romanization without conflating that choice with identity.
- Turkish and Ukrainian remain unresolved for lack of substantive target-language evidence.
