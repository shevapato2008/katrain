## Identity anchor and same-name risk

The [Nihon Ki-in official player profile](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000009.htm), observed in Japanese, identifies `林 海峯（リン カイホウ / LIN, Hai Fong）`, male, professional 9-dan, born 1942-05-06 in Shanghai, affiliated with Nihon Ki-in Tokyo, pupil of Go Seigen, and Honorary Tengen. This combination of Japanese professional affiliation, rank, birth date, Shanghai origin, and Go Seigen connection strongly identifies the intended Go professional. The [Nihon Ki-in 2nd Meijin history](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/meijin/002.htm) independently names `林 海峯九段` as challenger and 1977 Meijin champion.

The raw Chinese name is not unique. Chinese Wikipedia's disambiguation page [林海峰](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9E%97%E6%B5%B7%E5%B3%B0) lists, among others, this Go player, Hong Kong DJ/host/singer 林海峰, a Chinese naval band member, a 1969-born local official, and a 1975-born entrepreneur. A separate namesake associated with longevity and fasting has also appeared in Chinese search results. Do not link the 1,669 slots from raw name alone; use game dates/opponents and the professional Go identity above.
