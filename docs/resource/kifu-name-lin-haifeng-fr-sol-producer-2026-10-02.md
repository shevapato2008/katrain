The [French Wikipedia revision 228776821](https://fr.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Rin_Kaiho&oldid=228776821) titles the subject `Rin Kaiho`; [Wikidata Q707612](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q707612) has the exact French label `Rin Kaiho`; the French-language [FFG-linked game collection](https://www.jeudego.org/_php/rejoue_partie.php?s=demqrieqepcf) uses `Rin Kaiho` in its 1966 Meijin final, and the [Revue française de go](https://rfg.jeudego.org/item/les-inf-go-rmations-du-06-avril-2025/) uses `Rin Kaiho 9p (Tengen honoraire)` in its April 2025 Hall of Fame report. The FFG home page itself was checked, but it is not a player profile.

The [Nihon Ki-in official profile](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000009.htm) anchors the person as `林　海峯（リン　カイホウ / LIN, Hai Fong）`, professional 9-dan, born 1942-05-06 in Shanghai, pupil of Go Seigen, affiliated with Nihon Ki-in Tokyo. French Wikipedia gives the same birth date/place, teacher (`Wu Qing Yuan`), affiliation, and professional Go context. The 1966 Meijin and 1989 Tengen FFG-linked game records, and the RFG Honorary Tengen item, fit that player. The raw `林海峰` string alone does not identify a game slot; the Hong Kong entertainer namesake must remain separate until opponent/date/competition evidence identifies each slot.

## Excluded display forms and search leads

- `Lin Hai Fong` is sourced by the French Wikipedia infobox and is close to the official profile's Roman-letter `LIN, Hai Fong`. It is retained as a **search alias lead**, but the article's own title and French Go usage above favor `Rin Kaiho` for display.
- `Lin Hai Feng` and `Rin Kai Ho` are explicitly equated in *Belgo 46*. Both are **search alias leads**; the PDF does not establish either as the leading French form.
- `Rin Kaihō` occurs in the French Wikipedia lead, with a macron. It is a **search alias lead**; the unaccented title, Wikidata French label, game collection, and RFG item favor `Rin Kaiho`.
- `Lin Haifeng`, `Lín Hǎifēng`, `林海峰`, and `林　海峯` may help identity searches, but no French display choice is inferred from pinyin or the raw Chinese string. Titles/ranks such as `9p` and `Tengen honoraire` are excluded from the person name.

The registered `ffg` host is `ffg.jeudego.org`; the game collection and RFG live on sibling/parent hosts. Consequently they are documented as additional primary French Go evidence in this memo, not mislabelled as `ffg` source checks in the registry-bound JSONL.
