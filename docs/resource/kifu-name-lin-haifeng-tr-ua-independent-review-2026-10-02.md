# 林海峰: independent Turkish/Ukrainian name review

Research date: 2026-10-02 (Asia/Shanghai). Scope: target-language evidence for Turkish (`tr`) and Ukrainian (`ua`) forms for the Go professional 林海峰. This memo is an independent research result, not approval of candidate records, slot identity, a player link, or any database change.

## Findings

| Language | Observed form | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Turkish (`tr`) | `Rin Kaiho` | **PASS as direct Turkish-language usage evidence; HOLD as a fully identified local-source candidate.** GoMagic's Turkish article [“Motivasyon”](https://gomagic.org/tr/motivation/) is in Turkish and introduces a Go book, *Golden Opportunities*, with the byline `Yazan: Rin Kaiho`. Its text describes the book's tesuji problems. The article establishes publication of this romanized form in Turkish Go prose. It does not itself give the player's Chinese characters, biography, or career identifiers. The GoMagic page `/tr/on-shusaku/` is indexed at the same Turkish path but its article body is English; its caption does identify a young Rin Kaiho beside Go Seigen. This English body is corroboration only, not additional Turkish evidence. A Go-specific identity chain can be made through the book attribution on the Russian Go Federation's [RusGoLib profile](https://rusgolib.gofederation.ru/RinKaiho), which identifies `Рин Кайхо (Lin Haifeng – Линь Хайфэн)` as born in Shanghai, a 9p pupil of Go Seigen, and separately lists *Golden Opportunities* among his books. The [Nihon Ki-in official profile](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000009.htm) independently confirms the pro: `林 海峯（リン カイホウ / LIN, Hai Fong）`, born 1942-05-06 in Shanghai, professional 9-dan, pupil of Go Seigen. This multi-source chain supports that the Turkish byline refers to the intended player, but Turkish evidence remains a single book-credit occurrence. |
| Ukrainian (`ua`) | `Рін Кайхо` | **PASS as observed Ukrainian Go-community use; HOLD as a conventional display-name candidate.** The UFGO forum interview [“Задачі” page 2, post 88633](https://forum.ufgo.org/viewtopic.php?p=88633) is written in Ukrainian. In answer to which Go books he recommends, a participant identified in the thread as a 2p player says: `В основному це книги японських майстрів 80-х років - Рін Кайхо, Ісіда Йосіо, Кобаясі Коічі, Чо Чікун.` (“Mostly these are books by Japanese masters of the 80s — Rin Kaiho, Ishida Yoshio, Kobayashi Koichi, Cho Chikun.”) This is meaningful Ukrainian Go-community evidence for the exact spelling `Рін Кайхо`, but is an interviewee's recollection, not an editorial biography or a federation naming standard. The Nihon Ki-in profile above independently anchors a Japanese Go professional with the matching reading `リン カイホウ`; the forum passage's context also puts the name among Japanese Go masters and their books. That establishes a plausible connection, not a full person-level match from the Ukrainian page alone. Do not derive `Лінь Хайфен` mechanically from Russian or pinyin: no Ukrainian source for that alternate form was captured. |

Turkish searches surfaced the GoMagic Turkish book entry, an English-language GoMagic article under `/tr/`, and no stronger GoMagic/TGOD/Go Okulu biography in these queries. Ukrainian search surfaced the UFGO interview, but no stronger local biography in these exact-term/domain queries. These are search-result observations only; they do not establish absence or close the registered source scope. I did not find or capture an exact `uk` Wikidata label/alias or Ukrainian Wikipedia article in this pass.

## Offline candidate decision and ambiguity risk

The independently supported forms are `Rin Kaiho` (`tr`, retain Latin script) and `Рін Кайхо` (`ua`, retain observed Ukrainian spelling). **Neither should be written as approved yet:** Turkish evidence is a single book credit whose player link depends on a cross-source authorship chain; Ukrainian evidence is a single community interview mentioning Go books without biography data. Treat them as source-supported proposals for a later reviewer, not generated transliterations. Do not silently substitute the more Chinese-reading form `Lin Haifeng` or any Cyrillic rendering.

The raw value `林海峰` is ambiguous. The Chinese disambiguation page [林海峰](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9E%97%E6%B5%B7%E5%B3%B0) lists the Go professional and also Hong Kong DJ/host/singer 林海峰, among other namesakes. Thus the 1,669 raw-name slots cannot be bound to the Go professional from the name alone. Any slot-level link requires game dates/opponents or other Go-specific context. The official Nihon Ki-in identity anchor is: 林海峯, reading Rin Kaiho, 9-dan, born Shanghai 1942-05-06, pupil of Go Seigen.
