## Identity and source-language name

The match to Korean Go professional Yoo Chang-hyuk is high confidence. The [Korea Baduk Association profile](https://www.baduk.or.kr/record/player_view.asp?pkey=10000003) gives `유창혁(劉昌赫) 9단`, birth date 1966-04-25, Korean affiliation, and a career list including the Fujitsu, Ing, Samsung, Chunlan, and LG titles. The [Nihon Ki-in 2019 World Go Championship player profile](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/special/wgc/player.html) independently prints `劉 昌赫 ユ・チャンヒョク Yoo Chang-Hyuk`, identifies him as Korean, gives the same birth date and professional milestones, and lists the same five international title wins. This combination distinguishes him from other people with similar romanizations.

Source-language spelling and reading observed:

- Hangul: `유창혁`, used by the Korea Baduk Association.
- Hanja: `劉昌赫` on the Korean profile; `劉昌赫` in other sources. The first glyph is a compatibility form; preserve the exact glyph shown by each source when recording evidence.
- Japanese phonetic rendering: `ユ・チャンヒョク`, explicitly paired with `劉 昌赫` by Nihon Ki-in. This is direct reading evidence, not a Korean phonetic transcription.
- Revised Romanization: `Yu Changhyeok` appears in the English Wikipedia infobox; established international Go sources also witness `Yoo Changhyuk` / `Yoo Chang-Hyuk`. Do not infer one spelling from the other.
