## Identity and edition boundary

The target is the Japanese professional Go series 日本棋院選手権戦, launched as 第1期 in 1954 and ending with the 22nd edition in 1975. The Nihon Ki-in's official timeline documents the first edition in 1954; its historical match record uses the event name 日本棋院選手権戦. CWI's SGF archive independently identifies the title series as Nihon Ki-in Championship (日本棋院選手権戦), lists editions 1–22 across 1954–1975, and says it was succeeded by Tengen. The reported 1,153 games is the requested pinned product-group count, not a count asserted by either reference archive.

Keep this historical series distinct from the Kansai Ki-in Championship / 関西棋院選手権戦, the Kansai Ki-in 1st Place Tournament / 関西棋院第一位決定戦, and general uses of “Nihon Ki-in” as the organizing association. The first of those Kansai events had a separate history and was merged with the Nihon Ki-in series in forming Tengen; that later successor does not make the pre-1975 source series interchangeable. Also avoid erroneous secondary pages that assign post-1975 champions or 24 editions to this series.

## Review notes

- Keep the series core separate from edition numbers, title-holder labels, and dated source variants. The numbered series runs from 1 (1954) through 22 (1975), while individual games can be dated across multiple calendar years.
- `日本棋院選手権戦` is the official Japanese event name. The English source title `Nihon Ki-in Championship` is supported by CWI; avoid shortening it to `Nihon Ki-in`, which names the association.
- Do not combine with `関西棋院選手権戦` (Kansai Ki-in Senshuken) or `関西棋院第一位決定戦` (Kansai Ki-in 1st Place). They are distinct event series despite the Tengen-era merger/succession relationship.
- Chinese Simplified, Chinese Traditional, and Korean have language-page attestations. German, Spanish, French, Russian, Turkish, and Ukrainian candidates above are proposed translations only; keep them explicitly pending until localized evidence or editorial approval is supplied.
