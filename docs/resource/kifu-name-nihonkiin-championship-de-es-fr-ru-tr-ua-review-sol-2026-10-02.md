# Nihon Ki-in Championship: six-locale source review (2026-10-02)

Independent, read-only review of the `de`, `es`, `fr`, `ru`, `tr`, and `ua` rows left pending in [the eleven-language source memo](kifu-name-nihonkiin-championship-11lang-source-memo-2026-10-02.md). These are candidate display names for editorial consideration, not approved names or production changes. Pages were consulted on 2026-10-02.

## Fixed identity and exclusion boundary

The target is **日本棋院選手権戦**, the 22-edition Japanese professional Go series whose title results run **1954–1975**, not the Nihon Ki-in association itself. The [Nihon Ki-in's historical title record](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/docs/5renpa_20100825.pdf) names `日本棋院選手権戦`; [CWI's dedicated results](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/NKCh/) give `Nihon Ki-in Championship`, the Japanese name, the first and last editions, and Tengen as successor. [CWI's tournament index](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/index.html) separately lists `Nihon Ki-in Championship` (1954–1975), `Nihon Ki-in 1st` (1959–1968), and `Kansai Ki-in 1st`. The Kansai Ki-in Championship/関西棋院選手権戦 is also a distinct historical series. The later Tengen relationship does not collapse those series into one name. The pinned product count of 1,153 games is outside the source-name evidence reviewed here.

## Decision boundary

Four locales now have a direct target-language mention (`es`, `fr`, `ru`, `tr`), with stronger Go-community grounding in Turkish and corroborating but variant Russian Go-community text. German and Ukrainian remain **bounded absence** findings: the searches above did not locate the series, which does not establish that no such usage exists. None of the six strings is self-approved as a canonical display name or alias. The event must remain distinct from the association, `Nihon Ki-in #1`/日本棋院第一位決定戦, both Kansai series, and Tengen.

No code, database, production, or translation-table changes were made; this file was not staged or committed.
