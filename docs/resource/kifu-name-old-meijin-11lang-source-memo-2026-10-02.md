# Old Meijin name research: 11 languages (2026-10-02)

Read-only language evidence for the Japanese Yomiuri-sponsored Go title series played 1962–1975. This is the historical series called 旧・名人戦 in Nihon Ki-in materials, and must remain distinct from the post-1974/1976 Asahi Meijin series. These are source leads, not canonical-name approval.

| Language | Source-supported form | Evidence and status |
|---|---|---|
| en | **Old Meijin** | Direct title used by the CWI Go game archive, which explicitly describes the Yomiuri-sponsored series as 1962–1975 and links the later Asahi Meijin separately: [CWI Old Meijin archive](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/OMeijin/). Strong English form. |
| cn | **旧名人战** | Simplified Chinese Wikipedia article on the Japanese Go Meijin identifies the Yomiuri period as 旧名人战 and lists it separately from 新名人战: [名人战 (日本围棋)](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/名人战_(日本围棋)). Language-specific secondary evidence; not official Chinese naming. |
| tw | **舊名人戰** | Traditional Chinese Wikipedia article likewise calls the Yomiuri-era series 舊名人戰 and distinguishes the new series: [名人戰 (日本圍棋)](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/名人戰_(日本圍棋)). Language-specific secondary evidence; not official Taiwan naming. |
| jp | **旧・名人戦** | Nihon Ki-in's official chronology captions archival images 第2期旧名人戦 / 第4期旧名人戦; official player record also says 第14期旧名人戦: [Nihon Ki-in chronology](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/profile/enkaku/), [Otake record](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/player/htm/ki000008.htm). Direct primary evidence. |

## Identity boundary

Nihon Ki-in's modern archive labels the Asahi event simply 名人戦 and dates its founding to 1976: [modern Meijin archive](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/meijin/). CWI explicitly distinguishes the 1962–1975 Yomiuri Old Meijin from the later Asahi Meijin. Therefore, source records using bare “Meijin”/名人戦 need provenance or edition context before assignment to this legacy series. Note that historic sources differ on the initial numbering/starting year; this memo does not normalize editions.

No canonical IDs, translations, aliases, database records, or code were changed.
