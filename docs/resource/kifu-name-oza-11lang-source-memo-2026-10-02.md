# Japanese Oza series: 11-language source memo

Read-only source research, 2026-10-02. Scope is the Japanese professional Go 王座戦 series organized by the Nihon Ki-in, Kansai Ki-in, and Nikkei. This records source attestations and candidates only; it does not approve a registry or database value.

## Identity anchor and disambiguation

The [current Nihon Ki-in listing](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/062.html) identifies the event as 王座戦, names the Nihon Keizai Shimbun, Nihon Ki-in, and Kansai Ki-in as organizers, and describes the five-game title match. The [archived Nihon Ki-in English page](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/index-e.html) explicitly names it “Oza Title” and repeats those organizers, the title-match format, and its 1952 founding date. This is the exact Japanese Go series.

王座戦 is also the name of a shogi title. The Japan Shogi Association's [王座戦 page](https://www.shogi.or.jp/match/ouza/) is a shogi event and must not serve as identity evidence here. Likewise, translations of the title stem alone (王座战 / 王座戰 / 왕좌전) can be ambiguous. Accept localized-name evidence only where surrounding text identifies Japanese Go, the Japanese event, or its organizers/players.

## Limits

Names in cn/tw/ko need a Japan qualifier or clear event context because 王座 / 왕좌 also names shogi titles. The en form comes directly from the Nihon Ki-in. de and ru have Go-specific sources that identify the Japanese event. es/fr/tr/ua remain pending after this bounded search; the suggested forms are transliteration/translation candidates only and are not approved display values.
