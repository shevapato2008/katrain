Read-only follow-up on 2026-10-02 to [the 11-language source memo](kifu-name-oza-11lang-source-memo-2026-10-02.md). This is source assessment, **not** candidate approval, a registry entry, or a database change. The subject is the Japanese professional Go 王座戦, not the shogi 王座戦, the World Students Go Oza Championship, or another country's title.

## Identity control

The Nihon Ki-in's [Japanese event page](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/062.html) names 王座戦. Its [English event page](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/index-e.html) names **Oza Title**, lists the Nihon Keizai Newspaper, Nihon Ki-in and Kansai Ki-in, and describes the best-of-five title match. These facts are the identity anchor for the language checks below. The English proper name also supplies a conservative Latin-script rule basis where an independently localized name is unavailable.

## Bounded search and exclusions

The search used exact and contextual forms including `Oza` with Japanese Go, Iyama/Shibano, tournament/title terms in Spanish, French and Turkish; and Ukrainian `Одза`, `Оза`, `Оодза`, `王座`, Go/ґо/го, Japan and Nihon Ki-in. It checked Go Magic's language-specific pages, Spanish/French Go and encyclopedia pages, the Turkish Go Association, and `ufgo.org`/`forum.ufgo.org`. This is **bounded non-discovery** for Ukrainian, not an exhaustive negative closure across every registered source.

The [Turkish Go Association's student championship article](https://www.tgod.org.tr/avrupa-ogrenci-go-sampiyonasi-2026-izmirde-kayitlar-basladi/) mentions a **GO OZA Şampiyonası** qualification, which concerns the World Students event, not the Nihon Ki-in professional title. Spanish [Iberian student qualification](https://fedibergo.org/noticias/263-eliminatoria-a-24th-world-student-go-oza-championship) and the Ukrainian federation forum's English invitations have the same mismatch. The shared `Oza` stem alone is insufficient for identity. Shogi 王座戦 is likewise excluded.
