## Identity controls

For Oza, the [Nihon Ki-in Japanese page](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/062.html) gives `王座戦` and its Nikkei, Nihon Ki-in, and Kansai Ki-in organizers. The [Nihon Ki-in English archive](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/index-e.html) calls that event `Oza Title` and repeats the organizers and five-game title format. A shogi 王座戦, the World Students Go Oza Championship, and other `王座` titles are distinct.

For Old Meijin, the [Nihon Ki-in results PDF](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/z-obizaka/7final.pdf), page 1, explicitly labels the 12th–14th editions `旧・名人戦`. The [CWI Old Meijin archive](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/OMeijin/) names the Yomiuri-sponsored series, lists editions 1–14 with result years 1962–1975, and says that the Asahi-sponsored event received new numbering in 1976. The first edition includes games from 1961, so the result-year range is not an exclusion rule for game dates. The Korean, Taiwanese, and historical/classical Meijin titles also require context.

## Remaining evidence and decision limits

There are **10 source PASS and 1 source HOLD per series**. The two Ukrainian HOLDs concern **direct attestation only**; they do not reverse the existing project editorial approvals of generated spelling. Both generated choices still need a finite, object-specific negative-source closure and independently bound generated review before they can be treated as controlled import evidence. The [review runbook](kifu-name-review-runbook.md) specifies the exact `owner × lang × source_lang × scope` boundary, target-language official/Go plus encyclopedia or exact Wikidata checks, bounded scan/pagination and known-lead retention, source-body and scope hashes, and a subsequent independent `generated_review`. The [source registry](kifu-name-source-registry.json) still has `ua.complete_for_negative_claims: false`; the bounded searches in the memos and editorial notes are not that closure. Any other *new* mechanically shortened or translated form, including `Torneo Oza`, `Tournoi Oza`, `Oza unvanı`, bare `Eski Meijin`, or `Старий Мейдзин`, likewise has no source PASS here.

Go Magic's language pages are translations from one site, and player tables/forum pages show usage rather than official nomenclature. This review is limited to the cited forms and page bodies; it does not establish regional prevalence or rank variants as canonical. No candidate record, source registry, code, existing document, database row, or commit was changed for this review.
