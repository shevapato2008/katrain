The formal Japanese event name is **王座戦** (Ōza-sen / Ōza). The Nihon Ki-in's [69th Ōza record](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/oza/069.html) labels the event 棋戦名称「王座戦」, lists the hosts as 日本経済新聞社、日本棋院、関西棋院, and distinguishes 予選B・C, 本戦, and 挑戦手合五番勝負. Its current event summary also says the winner of a 16-player tournament enters the title challenge. The page currently displays current-cycle summary material as well as the 69th archive; it is used here for the stable name, organizer, and stage distinctions, not as evidence that all historical formats were identical.

Identity and history are corroborated by the revisioned [English Wikipedia article, revision 1342773010](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=%C5%8Cza_(Go)&oldid=1342773010), which identifies the Japanese professional Go title tournament, Nihon Ki-in organizer, Nihon Keizai Shinbun sponsor, and a preliminary winner progressing to a best-of-five match. The [Traditional Chinese Wikipedia article, revision 94023554](https://zh.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=%E7%8E%8B%E5%BA%A7%E6%88%B0_(%E6%97%A5%E6%9C%AC%E5%9C%8D%E6%A3%8B)&oldid=94023554) calls it one of Japan's seven major Go titles, founded in 1953 and jointly hosted by 日本經濟新聞社、日本棋院、關西棋院; it describes the preliminary tournaments, 本戰, and challenger/titleholder match. As an independent Go identity check, [GoMagic's Oza entry](https://gomagic.org/go-term/oza/) calls it one of Japan's seven major professional Go titles, gives Japanese 王座, Korean 왕좌, its 1953 establishment, and the single-elimination preliminary followed by a best-of-five title match.

## Naming and stage recommendation for later review

Treat the formal event core as **Ōza / 王座戦**. Retain the qualifier as a separate stage concept: the source evidence distinguishes Japanese 予選 (preliminaries), 本戦 (main tournament), and 挑戦手合 (title match). The pinned Chinese raw labels combine an edition, event, and sometimes stage (for example, 第14期日本王座战预选第3轮); this research does not normalize those raw values or authorize any localization candidate.

Suggested currently evidenced display forms for consideration only: en `Ōza` (or policy-standardized `Oza`), cn `王座战` (optionally disambiguated `日本王座战`), tw `王座戰` (optionally `王座戰 (日本圍棋)` where ambiguity requires it), jp `王座戦`, ko `일본 왕좌전`, ru `Одза`. Keep de/es/fr/tr/ua unresolved until target-language sources establish conventional forms.
