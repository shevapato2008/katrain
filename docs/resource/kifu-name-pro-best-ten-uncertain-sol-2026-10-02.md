# Pro Best Ten: follow-up on uncertain names and dates (2026-10-02)

This supplements `kifu-name-pro-best-ten-11lang-source-memo-2026-10-02.md`. It records a bounded follow-up search, not a database change. “Attested” below means the exact string is visible in a source for this event; a source using the English name in a different-language article does **not** establish a translated local title.

## Corrections to the first memo

- **Korean is attested.** The [Korean Baduk Association player record for Cho Chikun](https://m.baduk.or.kr/record/gisa_info.asp?PRPL_CODE=20000020) says “1975년 : 제12기 프로십걸전 우승”; its game records also use `프로십걸전`. This matches the [CWI twelfth-edition winner](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/go/games/games/ProBest10/), Cho Chikun in 1975, so the identity is secure. Use **프로십걸전** as the source-exact Korean title. If the UI needs sponsor disambiguation, **아사히 프로십걸전** is a generated qualified display form, with `프로십걸전` retained as the attested core. The previous candidate **아사히 프로 십걸전** should not be described as source-attested; its spacing differs from the federation’s form.
- **“Held 1964–1975” describes edition finals, not necessarily the whole period of play.** CWI lists first through twelfth title results in 1964–1975. [Nihon Ki-in’s own record of Sakata Eio’s streak](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/news/etc/post_677.html) dates first-edition main-draw games to 1964-02-13 and 1964-02-27 and final games to 1964-04-07 and 1964-04-11. [Go4Go’s Rin Kaiho record](https://www.go4go.net/go/games/byplayer/36/1650) indexes a *first* Japanese Pro Best Ten round-one game on 1963-09-12. The 1963 start in the Chinese article thus has a plausible match-play interpretation; do not call it an error or merely speculate about an announcement. [Nihon Ki-in’s centenary timeline](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/special/100anniversary/) places “プロ十傑戦開始” under **1962**, creating a further unresolved discrepancy. It does not give a game date or explain what “start” means. For data descriptions use: **“12 title editions, 1964–1975; first-edition play is indexed in 1963.”** Do not claim an unqualified founding/start year without resolving the Nihon Ki-in 1962 entry.

## Identity checks and boundaries

The Korean federation entry is tied to **Cho Chikun’s 1975 twelfth-edition win**, matching CWI’s event table; this rules out the Japanese **十段戦** (Korean `십단전`) despite the similar “ten” wording. The relevant Japanese event is **プロ十傑戦**, distinct from **アマ十傑戦**, **日本棋院第一位決定戦**, and the Asahi-sponsored **名人戦** begun in 1976. CWI’s first-edition final and the Nihon Ki-in dated games agree on the 1964 final. Earlier indexed first-edition games are compatible with a 1963–64 playing season, but the 1962 timeline entry remains unexplained.

## Search boundary

This pass searched Japanese event names with 1962/1963/1964 and first-edition terms; Korean Baduk Association pages; federation/domain-qualified French, German and Spanish queries; and Russian, Turkish, and Ukrainian event-name or Go-tournament phrasing. Search hits were checked for tournament identity, not accepted merely because they contained “Asahi” or “ten.” No translated DE/ES/FR/RU/TR/UA title met the reliable-source threshold. The French and Russian occurrences above support English-name carry-through only.
