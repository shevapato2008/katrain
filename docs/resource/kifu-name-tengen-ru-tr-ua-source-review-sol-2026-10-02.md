# Japanese Tengen series: ru/tr/ua source review

Read-only naming research, 2026-10-02. Scope is the **Japanese** Nihon Ki-in/Kansai Ki-in 天元戦, identified by the [Nihon Ki-in Japanese listing](https://www.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/tengen/049.html) and its [English archive](https://archive.nihonkiin.or.jp/match/tengen/index-e.html) (“Tengen Title”; Nihon Ki-in and Kansai Ki-in sponsors). China’s Tianyuan and Korea’s Cheonwon are separate tournaments. This supplements [the 11-language memo](kifu-name-tengen-11lang-source-memo-2026-10-02.md); it does not approve or write a registry/database value.

## Bounded search and exclusions

Searched Japanese-series title combinations in Russian (`Тэнгэн`/`Тенген` + Япония/Нихон Киин/турнир го), Turkish (`Tengen` + Japon/Japonya/Nihon Ki-in/turnuvası/unvanı, including Turkish Go organization and school sites), and Ukrainian (`Тенґен`/`Тенген` + Японія/Ніхон/турнір/ґо, including the Ukrainian Go Federation forum). Opened or examined the results above. General board-point definitions, the Ukrainian club, Japanese *Tengen* era, anime characters, and China–Japan/Korea title matches were excluded because they do not name the domestic Japanese 天元戦. Search-engine coverage is finite; “not found” for `ua` is not proof that no Ukrainian usage exists.
