## Scope of this finding

The evidence improves source coverage for this one player only. It does not choose final UI names, approve aliases for insertion, establish a general transliteration rule, or resolve the full eleven-language name catalog. In particular, the Korean primary form and Ukrainian specialist corroboration are review items. Source quotations above are limited to exact names and short identifiers; linked pages provide the context.
